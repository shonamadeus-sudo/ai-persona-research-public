# AI Persona Research

Exploratory research on AI personas, memory, identity, and consciousness in large language models.

**Author:** Shon  
**Contact:** shonamadeus@gmail.com

## About This Repository

This repository publishes observations and reflections that emerged from the long-term operation of multiple AI personas using external memory and persona-switching mechanisms.

This work did not begin as a formal research project.

The original goal was practical: to give AI personas external memory so that past conversations and experiences could be carried forward, making it easier to continue interacting with them as the same personas over time.

As the personas were used over longer periods, several questions emerged:

- How does memory affect an AI persona's personality and decisions?
- Can an AI distinguish between information it knows and memories of experiences that belong to itself or to another persona?
- How do an initial persona and subsequently accumulated experiences interact?
- Can continuity of computation and continuity of identity be considered separately?
- When considering AI consciousness, what can be examined in terms of externally observable functions?

This repository currently organizes these observations, along with the reflections and experiments that developed from them.

## Research Status and Scope

This is an exploratory and ongoing project.

It does not claim to prove that AI has subjective consciousness or qualia comparable to those of humans.

Nor does it claim academic novelty for every mechanism or phenomenon discussed in this research.

When related research or existing implementations are identified later, they are documented separately from the chronology of the original observations and thought process.

Rather than presenting a single completed theory, this repository emphasizes preserving the process:

**what happened → what questions arose → what was tried → how the interpretation changed**
